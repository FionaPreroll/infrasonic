# Starter presets

24 MilkDrop presets from the "Cream of the Crop" collection curated by ISOSCELES
(https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-cream-of-the-crop at commit 0180df21f5e0), as distributed by the
projectM project. Most MilkDrop presets were released without a licence; the projectM project treats them as being
in the public domain and removes presets on request of their authors.

They were chosen by `scripts/preset_bundle.py starter` of https://github.com/FionaPreroll/lactea: presets that load
without errors, need no textures and are cheapest to render, across the collection's categories. The full collection
is an optional download (Settings › Visualizer).
